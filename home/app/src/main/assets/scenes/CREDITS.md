# Couch layout scenes

The five scenes (`night`, `golden`, `water`, `fog`, `dawn`) come from couch-tv
(https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv, MIT licence), `tv-home/web/backgrounds/`.

couch-tv's credits say they "were made for couch-tv with an AI image model from text only (no source
photo). They are not real places. Use them however you like." The same terms apply to the files made from
them: `<name>-fg.webp`, the scenery cut out with the sky transparent, and `<name>-glass.jpg`, a blurred,
brightened copy that fills the clock.

The clock's typeface is Antonio Light (`res/font/antonio_light.ttf`), Copyright 2013 The Antonio Project
Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see `FONT-LICENSE-OFL.txt`).
